# Privacy Policy

AdvantageTune is a volunteer-run project that is owned and published by the Littleton STEM Educational Foundation ("Littleton Robotics"), a non-profit organization based in the United States.

_This policy was last modified on September 12th, 2026._

## Information We Collect

Littleton Robotics does not collect data from users of the AdvantageTune application.

## Third-Party Data Collection

The AdvantageTune repository is hosted by GitHub, which publishes a [privacy policy](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) that applies when interacting with their services.
